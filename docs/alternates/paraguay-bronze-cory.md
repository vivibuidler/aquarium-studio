### How it could work

Ten black neons, ten black phantoms and six bronze corys are a plausible peaceful community. The saved 23–25°C keeping scenario lies within the cory’s published range, but actual stock and water still need checking. Keep accessible clean sand, cover, good oxygen exchange and dedicated sinking food. Six corys meets the published minimum social-group recommendation. ### Why it stayed an alternate

This exact 10 + 10 + 6 combination was added later as a research option; its full cost, maintenance and combined adult-space assessment were not completed. The older rejected Paraguay prototype used different tetras, so its cost result does not establish this combination’s cost. Regional records labelled Corydoras aff. aeneus do not securely identify ordinary shop bronze corys as the same local fish.
