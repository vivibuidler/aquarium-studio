## Reviewed overview — 2026-10-06

A field-supported regional community whose original procurement route failed the budget screen.

**Bioload assessment:** Light to moderate expected adult load for the 30 small fish plus a modest ramshorn colony. Feeding access is a stronger concern than fish count alone.

### How it could work

Ten glowlights, ten head-and-tail-lights and ten dwarf pencilfish have a plausible peaceful combination. An Upper Essequibo survey records all three in the small forest creek habitat category; that supports the regional concept, not a single jointly sampled patch. Provide mature water, shaded planted cover, gentle circulation and food small enough for pencilfish. [Upper Essequibo field survey](https://www.scielo.br/j/bn/a/gxdzHNNbGR5KFBmVMxytPdM/) [Dwarf pencilfish husbandry](https://www.seriouslyfish.com/species/nannostomus-marginatus/)

### Why it stayed an alternate

The archived complete setup was $1,140.37, exceeding the $1,000 retail ceiling by $140.37. That was a procurement rejection, not proof of overcrowding or incompatible species. Pencilfish food access remained uncertain, and the historical care estimate of 53.97–66.73 minutes/week exceeded the 65-minute target at its upper end. The two-species main plan removed the pencilfish feeding task and reduced cost.

### What needs checking

Observe pencilfish eating alongside both tetra groups; their timid nature can make competition important. Confirm manageable tiny prepared/frozen food acceptance, Mayaca growth and pruning under the installed light, and a revised complete quote. Mayaca establishment also needs checking in the main plan. Snail shell health needs particular attention if using soft, acidic water. [Pencilfish diet and temperament](https://www.seriouslyfish.com/species/nannostomus-marginatus/)

All three assessments now include your ramshorns. A modest colony adds some load; a growing, heavily fed colony adds more. Check stable pH and mineral availability for shell health alongside the fish requirements. Low bioload alone does not settle feeding, comfort or biotope accuracy.
